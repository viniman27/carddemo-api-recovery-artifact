# Research questions are run-specific

The framework does not select a study's RQs, comparison arms, sample sizes or hypotheses. Declare them in the new study/run protocol before collection, under researcher approval. No results or provisional RQ numbering from account-balance or historical V4 are evidence for a new run.

Useful dimensions to consider, not an approved question set: evidence grounding; adequacy of the recovered contract; human interventions; observable obligations and validation limits; execution cost; transferability under a changed backend and explicit setup protocol.

Auditability is a property to inspect; fidelity or superiority require corresponding evidence. No page in this steering directory establishes that an empirical hypothesis was confirmed or rejected for the current application. Historical formulations and findings remain preserved in `../../baseline/steering/research-questions.md`.

The AWS public cycle and future Phase 2 have separate corpora and may use different pipeline versions. Publication planning does not authorize execution or collapse those cycles.
